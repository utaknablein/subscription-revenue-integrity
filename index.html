From the number to a decision

VALUE is the five-step method I use to turn a suspicious renewal rate into a tested change.

  1. VVerify the customer problemTalk to subscribers and finance. Look at billing awareness, engagement and complaints.
  2. AAssess the economicsSeparate headline renewal from full-price renewal, discounts and net contribution.
  3. LLaunch a fair experimentTest transparent renewal experiences and incentives with random assignment.
  4. UUnderstand the evidenceSet profit and trust guardrails in advance. Read the result with its uncertainty.
  5. EExpand or exitScale what the evidence supports. Stop when trust, compliance or economics slip.
